أسئلةQuestions

Good to know

Umrah from your town: Thiruvananthapuram · Kochi · Kollam

How is a tailor-made Umrah priced?

Every quote is built from your dates, number of days, hotel distance and room sharing. You get the full fare in writing before you pay anything.

When are the group departures?

Groups with an Ameer leave every four months, in March, July and November. Message us to hold a seat on the next one.

What documents do I need for an Umrah visa?

A passport valid for at least six months, a recent photo on a white background and any vaccination certificate currently required. Rules change, so we send you the up-to-date checklist when you enquire.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. You pay an advance to hold your seat and the balance before the visa is filed. We give a written receipt for every payment.
