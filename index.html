أسئلةQuestions

Questions pilgrims ask us most

What documents do I need for an Umrah visa?

A passport valid for at least six months, a recent photo on a white background and any vaccination certificate currently required. Rules change, so we send you the up-to-date checklist when you enquire.

How long does the Umrah visa take?

Usually a few working days once your documents are complete. Apply at least three to four weeks before you plan to fly, and earlier for Ramadan.

Can women travel for Umrah without a mahram?

Saudi rules on this have changed in recent years. Tell us who is travelling and we will explain what applies today and how our groups support women travellers.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. You pay an advance to hold your seat and the balance before the visa is filed. We give a written receipt for every payment.

Is the price per person?

Yes. Prices are per person and depend on room sharing, the season and your departure airport. We confirm the exact fare in writing before you pay.

Do you help elderly pilgrims?

Yes. We arrange wheelchairs on request, keep elders in hotels with short walks, and our guide checks on them every day.
